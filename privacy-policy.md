# RILAW Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: September 21, 2026**

RILAW ("RILAW", "we", "us", or "our") is a human rights and international law application designed to provide users with access to legal and human rights information, educational resources, news, documents, cases, treaties, events, and related digital services.

This Privacy Policy explains how RILAW collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the RILAW application and related services.

## 1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, RILAW may process the following information:

### Account Information

If you choose to sign in using Google or LinkedIn, we may receive information provided by the relevant authentication provider, including:

* Name
* Email address
* Profile photo
* Provider-specific account identifier
* Authentication information required to establish your RILAW account

We do not receive or store your Google or LinkedIn password.

### User-Generated Information

RILAW may store information that you create or save while using the application, including:

* Bookmarks
* Notes
* Recently viewed items
* Selected preferences and settings
* Notification preferences
* Other information associated with your RILAW account

Some user information may be stored locally on your device to support offline functionality.

## 2. Authentication Providers

RILAW may support authentication through third-party identity providers, including:

* Google
* LinkedIn

When you use a third-party provider to authenticate, that provider processes information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

RILAW only requests information reasonably necessary to create and maintain your account.

## 3. Firebase

RILAW uses Google Firebase services to support authentication, cloud data storage, synchronization, and application infrastructure.

Depending on the features available in the application, Firebase services may process:

* Firebase user identifiers
* Authentication provider information
* Account information
* Application data associated with your account

Firebase data is used to provide and maintain RILAW's functionality and to synchronize information between supported devices and services.

## 4. LinkedIn Authentication

If LinkedIn authentication is available and you choose to use it, RILAW may process information received through LinkedIn's OpenID Connect authentication system, including your LinkedIn account identifier, name, email address, and profile information made available through the authorization process.

RILAW does not request or store your LinkedIn password.

LinkedIn authentication may use a secure server-side authentication component operated through Cloudflare Workers. Sensitive credentials required for authentication are intended to remain on the server-side infrastructure and are not embedded in the RILAW mobile application.

## 5. Cloudflare

RILAW may use Cloudflare Workers as a serverless infrastructure component for authentication and related application services.

Cloudflare Workers may process technical authentication requests, including OAuth authorization responses and authentication information necessary to establish a RILAW session.

Sensitive credentials, such as OAuth client secrets, are intended to be stored as protected server-side secrets and are not intentionally exposed to application users.

## 6. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

* Create and maintain your RILAW account
* Authenticate users
* Synchronize user data
* Provide bookmarks, notes, preferences, and other personalized features
* Provide application functionality and content
* Maintain application security
* Prevent unauthorized access and abuse
* Improve reliability and performance
* Respond to user requests and support inquiries

We do not sell your personal information.

## 7. Data Storage

RILAW may use both local and cloud-based storage.

Some information may be stored locally on your device to support offline functionality. Other account-related information may be stored using Firebase or other infrastructure required to provide RILAW services.

The specific information stored may depend on the features you use and the authentication method you choose.

## 8. Data Sharing

RILAW does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that are necessary to operate the application, including authentication, cloud infrastructure, hosting, security, and application service providers.

These providers process information only as necessary to provide their services or as otherwise permitted by applicable law.

## 9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through RILAW.

Authentication credentials and sensitive server-side secrets are intended to be protected from direct access by application users.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested RILAW services, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Local data may remain on your device until it is removed by you or the application.

## 11. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your RILAW account and associated personal information.

Deletion requests may be submitted through the contact method provided below.

Where technically and legally possible, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with the account. Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

## 12. Children's Privacy

RILAW is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable privacy laws.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to RILAW without appropriate authorization, please contact us.

## 13. Third-Party Services

RILAW may rely on third-party services, including:

* Google Firebase
* Google Sign-In
* LinkedIn
* Cloudflare

These services have their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of the services they use.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to RILAW, its features, infrastructure, or applicable legal requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy will be updated.

## 15. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, data processing, or deletion of your information, please contact the RILAW project administrator through the official contact information associated with the RILAW project.
